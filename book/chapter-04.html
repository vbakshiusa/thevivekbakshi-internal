Meanwhile, in the World

🌍 Globally

  • The IBM PC (1981) and the Apple Macintosh (1984) put computers on desks everywhere.
  • Live Aid in 1985 became the first truly global broadcast concert.
  • The Falklands War and a hardening Cold War dominated headlines.

🇮🇳 In India

  • India won the Cricket World Cup at Lord’s in June 1983 — a moment that changed the country’s self-belief.
  • Colour television arrived with the 1982 Asian Games in Delhi.
  • Operation Blue Star, Indira Gandhi’s assassination and the Bhopal gas tragedy made 1984 one of India’s darkest years.

🇺🇸 In the USA

  • Reagan’s America boomed; MTV launched in 1981 and rewrote pop culture.
  • The AIDS crisis emerged and the Cold War arms race intensified.
