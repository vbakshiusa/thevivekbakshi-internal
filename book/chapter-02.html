Meanwhile, in the World

🌍 Globally

  • The Vietnam War ended in 1975 as Saigon fell.
  • The Iranian Revolution and the Soviet invasion of Afghanistan closed out 1979, triggering a second oil shock.
  • Personal computing was born: the Apple II arrived in 1977, the same year Star Wars and Voyager launched.

🇮🇳 In India

  • The Emergency (1975–77) suspended civil liberties; the Janata Party then won the first non-Congress government.
  • Sholay (1975) redefined Indian cinema and the Amitabh Bachchan era began.
  • India’s first test-tube baby was born in Kolkata in 1978.

🇺🇸 In the USA

  • Jimmy Carter’s presidency wrestled with stagflation and the Iran hostage crisis.
  • Disco, Star Wars and the first home computers defined the culture.
