Meanwhile, in the World

🌍 Globally

  • The Soviet Union dissolved in December 1991, ending the Cold War.
  • Nelson Mandela walked free in February 1990; Germany reunified that October.
  • Nirvana’s Nevermind (1991) upended rock, and Freddie Mercury died that November.

🇮🇳 In India

  • The 1991 balance-of-payments crisis forced the reforms that opened India’s economy.
  • Rajiv Gandhi was assassinated in May 1991.
  • Satellite television arrived — Star TV and Zee TV changed what India watched.

🇺🇸 In the USA

  • The Gulf War was the first conflict broadcast live around the clock.
  • The World Wide Web went public in 1991; Clinton was elected in 1992.
