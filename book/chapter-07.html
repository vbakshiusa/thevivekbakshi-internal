Meanwhile, in the World

🌍 Globally

  • The web exploded — Netscape, Yahoo and Amazon all arrived between 1994 and 1995.
  • Windows 95 made the internet mainstream.
  • Mandela became South Africa’s president in 1994; the Rwandan genocide and Bosnian war marked the decade’s worst.

🇮🇳 In India

  • The internet came to India on 15 August 1995 via VSNL.
  • Liberalization took hold — foreign brands, cable TV and a new consumer culture.
  • The 1993 Mumbai bombings and the 1996 Cricket World Cup bookended the years.

🇺🇸 In the USA

  • The dot-com boom began; the OJ Simpson trial gripped the country in 1995.
  • The Oklahoma City bombing in 1995 was the deadliest domestic attack to that point.
