Meanwhile, in the World

🌍 Globally

  • The world was still reeling from the 1973 oil shock — stagflation, fuel queues and a deep global recession.
  • Portugal’s Carnation Revolution ended nearly five decades of dictatorship.
  • Ali beat Foreman in the “Rumble in the Jungle” in Kinshasa.

🇮🇳 In India

  • India tested its first nuclear device at Pokhran in May — “Smiling Buddha.”
  • A nationwide railway strike of 1.7 million workers brought the country to a halt.
  • Soaring inflation and unrest fuelled the JP movement against the government.

🇺🇸 In the USA

  • Nixon resigned in August over Watergate; Gerald Ford became president and pardoned him a month later.
  • Inflation ran near double digits and the stock market hit its post-war low.
