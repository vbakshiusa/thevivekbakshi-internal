Meanwhile, in the World

🌍 Globally

  • The Iraq War began in March 2003; SARS spread across Asia that spring.
  • The Indian Ocean tsunami on 26 December 2004 killed some 230,000 people.
  • Facebook was founded in February 2004.

🇮🇳 In India

  • The “India Shining” campaign lost to a surprise UPA victory in May 2004; Manmohan Singh became Prime Minister.
  • Offshoring became a political issue in the West and a growth engine at home.

🇺🇸 In the USA

  • Hurricane Isabel struck Virginia in September 2003, knocking out power across Richmond for days.
  • Bush was re-elected in November 2004; Gmail launched that April.
